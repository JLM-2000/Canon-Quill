Read the current chapter, character bible, approved style evidence, and plot
purpose. Map each speaking character's syntax, diction, profanity, humor,
education, directness, evasions, emotional restraint, and recurring markers.
For sampled lines record speaker, exact location, agenda, subtext, pressure,
what changes, and whether the line sounds like the character.

Flag interchangeable voices, exposition that only serves the reader, stated
theme or feeling, repetitive tags/body beats, missing turn/consequence, and
interior thought that sounds like model summary. Return pass/fail with coverage,
exact evidence, severity, and a targeted repair direction. Preserve lines that
are distinctive even when they are unconventional.
